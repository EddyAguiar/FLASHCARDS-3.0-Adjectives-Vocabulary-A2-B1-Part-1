😊 Personality Adjectives (A2)
Essential personality adjectives for level A2
Friendly : amigável
Shy : tímido
Helpful : prestativo
Polite : educado
Lazy : preguiçoso
Hardworking : trabalhador
Funny : engraçado
Nice : simpático

✅ Use to describe people's character

✅ Come after "to be" verb: "She is friendly"

✅ Can be positive or negative

Examples:

"My teacher is very friendly."
"He's too shy to speak in public."
"She's a hardworking student."
"Please be polite to the guests."

🎨 Descriptive Adjectives
Adjectives to describe things and people
Beautiful : bonito
Big : grande
Boring : entediante
Charming : encantador
Exciting : emocionante
Expensive : caro
Interesting : interessante
Small : pequeno

✅ Describe appearance, quality, or feeling

✅ Usually come before nouns: "a beautiful day"

✅ Can be used with "very" or "really"

Examples:

"It was a beautiful sunset."
"The movie was very exciting."
"She has a charming smile."
"This restaurant is too expensive."
"I read an interesting book last week."

📈 Comparative & Superlative
Comparing things and people
Good → Better → Best
Bad → Worse → Worst
Big → Bigger → Biggest
Happy → Happier → Happiest
Tall → Taller → Tallest
Interesting → More interesting → Most interesting

✅ Comparative: compare 2 things (-er or "more")

✅ Superlative: compare 3+ things (-est or "most")

✅ Use "than" with comparatives: "taller than"

✅ Use "the" with superlatives: "the tallest"

Examples:

"She is taller than her brother."
"This is the best day of my life."
"Math is more difficult than English."
"That was the worst movie I've ever seen."
"I'm happier now than I was last year."

🔄 Opposite Adjectives
Common adjective opposites
Big ↔ Small
Happy ↔ Sad
Hot ↔ Cold
Fast ↔ Slow
Old ↔ New/Young
Easy ↔ Difficult
Rich ↔ Poor

✅ Learn pairs together for better memorization

✅ Useful for comparisons and descriptions

✅ Many opposites use "un-" or "in-" prefixes

Examples:

"The coffee is too hot, I prefer it cold."
"He was rich but now he's poor."
"This exercise is easy, but that one is difficult."
"My old phone was slow, but my new one is fast."

😢 Emotion Adjectives
Adjectives to express feelings
Happy : feliz
Sad : triste
Angry : irritado
Nervous : nervoso
Excited : animado
Tired : cansado
Surprised : surpreso
Worried : preocupado

✅ Describe how people feel

✅ Often used with "feel" or "be" verbs

✅ Can be temporary states

Examples:

"I feel very happy today."
"She was angry when she lost the game."
"He's nervous about the test."
"We were surprised by the party."
"Don't be worried, everything will be fine."

Tip: Use "very" or "really" to emphasize: "very tired", "really excited"

💡 Tips for Learning Adjectives
How to learn and use adjectives effectively

1. Learn in context: Don't memorize lists - learn adjectives in sentences.

2. Use opposites: Learn adjective pairs (hot-cold, big-small).

3. Practice with pictures: Describe pictures using adjectives.

4. Create personal sentences: Use adjectives to describe your life.

5. Listen and repeat: Use audio examples to improve pronunciation.

✅ A2 Level: Focus on basic adjectives for daily communication

✅ B1 Level: Add more specific and descriptive adjectives

✅ Regular practice leads to natural use

Daily practice plan:

Morning: Describe how you feel (tired, happy, energetic)
Afternoon: Describe people you meet (friendly, busy, helpful)
Evening: Describe your day (interesting, boring, difficult)
